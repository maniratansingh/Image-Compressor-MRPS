[FULL DESCRIPTION] (Max 4000 characters)
Image Compressor MB to KB helps you reduce large photo sizes directly on your Android phone. Compress JPG, PNG and WEBP images, crop photos to your desired aspect ratio, preview results before saving, and create smaller image files for online forms, email, documents, and storage saving.

Your photos stay 100% private. Compression happens directly on your device, meaning your images are never uploaded to any server or cloud.

WHY USE THIS APP?
• Reduce large photos from MB to small KB file sizes.
• Crop photos and adjust aspect ratios freely with an intuitive editor.
• Preview compressed images with a side-by-side comparison before saving.
• View real-time estimated file sizes while adjusting the quality slider.
• Enter an exact target file size (e.g., 50KB) for strict form requirements.
• Batch Compress multiple photos at once to save time.
• Free up valuable storage space on your phone.
• Supports JPG, JPEG, PNG (Lossless), and WEBP formats.
• Perfect for job applications, university forms, emails, and sharing.
