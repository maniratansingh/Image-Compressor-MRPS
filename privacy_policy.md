## Overview

Image Compressor – MRPS is an offline image compression utility for Android. This privacy policy explains how the application handles your data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any data.**

- No personal information is collected.
- No images are uploaded to any server.
- No usage analytics or telemetry data is collected.
- No advertising identifiers are used.
- No cookies or tracking technologies are used.

## Image Processing

All image compression is performed **locally on your device**. Your images are:

- Processed entirely on your phone's processor.
- Never transmitted over the internet.
- Never uploaded to any server or cloud service.
- Never shared with any third party.

The application works fully offline, including in Airplane Mode.

## Accounts and Authentication

The application does not require or support:

- User accounts
- Login or registration
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Any form of authentication

## Permissions

The application requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

- **Photo/media access**: To allow you to select images for compression. This uses Android's built-in Photo Picker, which does not grant the app broad access to your photo library.

The application does not request or use:

- Camera
- Microphone
- Location
- Contacts
- Phone
- SMS
- Internet access for its own operations

## Third-Party Services

The application does not integrate with any third-party services, including:

- Advertising networks
- Analytics platforms
- Crash reporting services
- Social media SDKs
- Cloud processing services

## Data Storage

Compressed images are saved to locations you choose on your device. Temporary files created during compression are stored in the app's private cache and are cleaned up automatically.

The application does not maintain a database of your images or compression history.

## Data Sharing

The application does not share any data with anyone. When you use the Android Share function to send a compressed image, the sharing is handled by Android's built-in sharing mechanism and the receiving application — not by Image Compressor.

## Children's Privacy

The application does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is a simple offline utility tool.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be reflected in the application and on the developer's website.
